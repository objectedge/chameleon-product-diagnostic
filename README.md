# Product diagnostic

A short self-assessment for founders, based on the fnSummit 2026 Chamaeleon talk. Answer 20 questions about your own product and get a readout by area with a ranked list of what to do first.

**Talk slides:** https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/14GV0Q4DqlpJ4vMRZwaZWSZc1eNmFmvtFFjOJqhSq5ZE/edit?usp=sharing

## Use it

Open the published page, enter your product name and a one-line description, and answer each question. At the end you can copy the summary, download it as a `.txt` file, or print it to PDF.

Answers stay in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

## Areas covered

1. The $20 test
2. Pricing
3. Model dependency
4. Timing: early or wrong
5. Moats
6. Team and judgment
7. Capital

Each answer scores as protected, partial or exposed. An area is Protected at 75% or above, Partial at 40â€“74%, and Exposed below 40%. The readout also flags a missing enabler, missing demand, fewer than two defensible moats, and an unknown model swap time.

## Files

- `index.html`: the diagnostic, a single self-contained file with no dependencies.
- `SKILL.md`: the same framework as a skill, so an AI assistant can interview someone about their product and produce the same readout.
